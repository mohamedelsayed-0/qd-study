==================================================================

[S1] "Performance optimization of efficient PbS quantum dots solar cells
     through numerical simulation", Scientific Reports 13, 10759 (2023).
     https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-023-36769-y
     Open access: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10310699/
     -> PbS-QD absorber (PbS-TBAI), p-PbS-EDT hole layer, MoO3, TiO2  (Table 1).
     This paper models the SAME PbS-QD / PbS-EDT / MoOx materials we use.

[S2] "Comprehensive Analysis for Low-Cost and Highly Efficient Perovskite
     Solar Cells Using SCAPS-1D with an Inexpensive Hole Transport Material,
     Electron Transport Material, and Back Contact Considering the Toxicity"
     (PMC, 2024/25).  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12409573/
     -> MAPbI3 absorber + ETL/HTL parameter table.

[S3] "Performance simulation of the perovskite solar cells with Ti3C2 MXene
     in the SnO2 electron transport layer" (PMC, 2024).
     https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10923826/
     -> SnO2 ETL  (Table 1).

[S4] FTO front-contact values are the consensus set used across SCAPS
     perovskite studies (FTO: Eg ~3.5 eV, chi 4.4 eV, work function 4.4 eV),
     e.g. "Numerical simulation and optimization of FTO/TiO2/CZTS/CuO/Au solar
     cell using SCAPS-1D", Sci. Rep. 15 (2025) s41598-025-12999-0.
     https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-12999-0

[S5] PROJECT (this work) — FDTD optical design.
     QD best = QD_1550_NH4I (Eg = 0.80 eV)
       -> data/generation/charge_best.mat
     Voltage hedge = QD_1200_NH4I (Eg = 1.03 eV)
       -> data/generation/charge_hedge.mat
     Layer thicknesses: FTO 300 / SnO2 40 / MAPbI3 550 / PbS-QD 300 /
     p-PbS-EDT 50 / MoOx 10 / Au 100 nm.
