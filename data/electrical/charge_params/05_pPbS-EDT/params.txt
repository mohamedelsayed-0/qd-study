LAYER 05 — p-PbS-EDT  (hole transport layer, HTL)
=================================================
Role        : hole transport / electron blocking (p-type PbS, EDT ligand)
Thickness   : 50 nm                            (design, S5;  S1 used 45 nm)

Eg          : 1.14 eV                           (S1)
chi (EA)    : 3.9  eV                            (S1)
eps_r       : 20                                (S1)
Nc          : 1e19 cm-3                          (S1)
Nv          : 1e19 cm-3                          (S1)
mu_n        : 2e-4 cm2/Vs                        (S1)
mu_p        : 20   cm2/Vs                        (S1)
N_D         : 1e14 cm-3                          (S1)
N_A (accept): 1e16 cm-3   (p-type)               (S1)
Nt (defect) : 1e14 cm-3                          (S1)

Note: EDT-treated PbS is p-type and acts as the hole-selective layer between the
QD absorber and MoOx. Strongly asymmetric mobility (mu_p >> mu_n) blocks electrons.
