LAYER 03 — MAPbI3  (perovskite, visible absorber)
=================================================
Role        : visible-light absorber (300-800 nm)
Thickness   : 550 nm                           (design, S5)

Eg          : 1.55 eV   (1.50 also common)     (S2 / consensus; matches FDTD)
chi (EA)    : 3.9  eV                           (S2)
eps_r       : 10   (some studies 6.5)          (S2)
Nc          : 2.75e18 cm-3                      (S2)
Nv          : 3.9e18  cm-3                      (S2)
mu_n        : 10   cm2/Vs   (sweep 1 ... 50)    (S2)
mu_p        : 10   cm2/Vs   (sweep 1 ... 50)    (S2)
N_A (accept): 1e15 cm-3     (lightly p-type; sweep 1e15 ... 1e18)  (S2)
N_D         : 0
Nt (defect) : 1e14 cm-3     (bulk SRH; sweep 1e13 ... 1e16)        (S2)
tau (SRH)   : ~1-100 ns     (set via Nt + capture cross-section; ~10 ns typical)

Note: bandgap of the actual MAPbI3 optical model = 1.55 eV. mu and Nt/tau are
the bulk sweep knobs per the project plan.
